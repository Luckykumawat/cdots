A few things you might be wondering.

How do I access a product?+

Choose a product and select “Open app”. It opens the product’s own website in a new tab. Some apps may ask you to sign in or connect an account.

Are all the apps free to use?+

Availability, access requirements and pricing are set by each product. Visit the individual app for its current details. For software without a public link, use “Ask about access”.

Can you build a website or software for my business?+

Yes. Services include website and e-commerce development, custom software, automation, UI/UX, SEO, marketplace management, social media, advertising and photography. Email your requirements to okuma.jaipur@gmail.com to discuss scope.

Will more products be added?+

Yes. This collection is designed to grow with new apps, websites and software. Come back to discover new additions.

Privacy on this website

Visitors can browse the catalogue without signing in. Search and filters run in your browser. The owner signs in with a private administrator account to edit the catalogue; product information and uploaded images are stored online. No analytics or tracking scripts have been added.

Opening an app takes you to a separate website with its own privacy practices. Email and phone links open your preferred communication app.
